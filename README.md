One line of weather, at most 140 characters, for a city that does not exist — judged blind by a bored commuter who picks the line that makes them smile. The $2 prize goes to 1st place. Free to enter.

This entry's forecast (128 characters, one line):

> The bus comes the moment you stop looking for it. Gloomsbury: sunny, 19°, a breeze, and the rain is running 10 minutes late too.

The punchline comes first — the bus arrives the moment you stop looking for it — then the city, its weather, and a second twist: the rain itself is running late too, so the weather shares the commuter's delay.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

It writes the forecast to `/output/entry.txt`.
